HOW IT WORKS

From request to execution in four steps.

1

Create the Order

An operator or dispatcher creates a switch order, defines the circuits involved, and writes out each step of the switching procedure.

2

Review & Approve

A supervisor reviews the order for safety and accuracy, then approves it so crews can proceed with full confidence and authorization.

3

Execute in the Field

Assigned crews execute each step in sequence, marking completion as they go. The entire team sees live progress from any device.

4

Archive & Comply

Completed orders are automatically archived with full timestamps and sign-offs — ready for audits, NERC compliance, or future reference.
